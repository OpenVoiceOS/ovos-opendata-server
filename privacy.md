## TigreGótico OpenData Privacy Policy  

> OpenData is an initiative to collect and publish datasets from OVOS real world usage, this data will be curated into permissively licensed datasets to benefit the broader community and directly improve OpenVoiceOS

### What We Collect  
If you **opt-in** to data collection, OVOS will send the following data:  
- Utterance text (what you said)  
- Intent classification (how OVOS understood it)  
- Language  

No personally identifiable information is collected.  

### How We Use This Data  
- To improve OVOS models and intent recognition.  
- The data **may**, and **probably will**, be shared publicly in a Hugging Face dataset for community research.  

### Your Choices  
- Data collection is **disabled by default**.  
- You can enable/disable it at any time in the settings.  

### Important Notes  
- Submitted data **cannot** be deleted, as we cannot verify the sender.  
- Data is **never sold** or used for advertising.  
- Point OVOS to **your own server**, your data, your rules!

By enabling data collection, you agree to these terms.
